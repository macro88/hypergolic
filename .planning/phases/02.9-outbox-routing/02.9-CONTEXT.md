## Publication integration questions still open

The Plan03 foundation has no admission, signer or send path. It supports both explicit app-only ancillary policies without choosing one by default. Before trusted publication integration, the owner needs to choose:

- Required inbox post-send receipts: require acceptance from every resolved recipient inbox relay for overall success, or accept any relay and expose incomplete required fanout. Recommendation: require every required inbox ACK, because the pinned draft describes toInboxes as a delivery contract. A missing ACK remains unknown; rejection remains rejected.
- Ancillary failure: retain primary-event success with a separate relay-list failure, or make the overall request fail if ancillary redistribution is incomplete. Recommendation: separate results, because the two events have independent receipts and accepted primary publication cannot be rolled back.
- Explicit-only toOutbox:false: omit own-list lookup/redistribution, or still discover and review the existing own list for redistribution to the selected destinations. Recommendation: omit, because the caller explicitly skips own routing and this avoids extra own-list discovery and publication.

These recommendations are pending, not accepted product policy. Current native inventory has no physical iPhone; supported protected iOS signing acceptance remains unavailable. Do not substitute a Simulator signer or claim Plan03 complete.

Conformance correction: the pinned draft says at most one signed event per outbox.publish request, not merely one newly signed event. The already accepted D-06 existing-list redistribution is a selected profile extension and must be documented as such.

## Publication decisions accepted — 5 October 2026

The owner answered A1 B1 C1 to the publication follow-up interview. These supersede the preceding pending status:

- D-07: Overall primary success requires acceptance from every required recipient inbox relay. Otherwise report failure with exact rejected/unknown receipts. When no required inbox exists, at least one primary relay acceptance is needed. Basis: requested inboxes are a delivery contract; acceptance at unrelated destinations cannot satisfy it. - D-08: Ancillary relay-list redistribution has independent receipts. Its failure does not reverse primary-event success. Basis: two distinct events have independent outcomes and primary acceptance cannot be rolled back.
- D-09: toOutbox:false skips own-list lookup and redistribution; required toInboxes and explicit destinations still apply. Basis: respect the explicit own-routing opt-out and avoid extra discovery/publication.

Native trusted review/sign/send integration may now proceed with these policies. Current physical iOS signing evidence remains unavailable; preserve that acceptance gap.
