# Confirmed lifecycle acceptance

Loaded feeds stay live across focus changes. Native background/inactivity first
revokes delivery and cancels sockets; logical reads and visible drafts remain
while the process survives. Foreground opens fresh operation/server IDs, refreshes
bounded recent history and deduplicates recent events without a gap-free promise.
Close, identity change, view replacement/navigation, renderer loss and termination
revoke old authority before cancellation. No terminal close is invented for pause.

Both final native live journeys pass eight checks, with unchanged source and
matching installed Release products. Switching keeps two connections and drafts;
background/return replaces those connections; explicit close and old-owner
revocation stop delivery; cold restart has zero old stream intent. Nine controlled
fault cases per platform include late delivery, paused-owner revocation, malformed
signature, foreign subscription, flood and oversize denials. A real process-owned
runtime identity transition independently cancels paused subscriptions.

The ordinary public-only published-update fixture retains its verified accepted
revision and connected runtime after background/return on Android and iOS. Approval
and pending transfer protections remain independent. An Android lifecycle crash
was fixed by closing raw TCP synchronously and leaving TLS close-notify on its
worker. A diagnostic iOS wrapper flattening issue was fixed with the same
non-collapsible wrapper already used by the production workspace.

Final full quality scores are 100/100, seven audits are clean, both native normal
Release platforms compile, and strict TypeScript/exports/prebuilds pass. The known
Expo Doctor mismatch remains; no pins or gates were lowered. See
[verification](02.2-VERIFICATION.md) for exact scopes and remaining acceptance.
CAP-03 is accepted for the selected profile; CAP-01 continues and CAP-14 stays open.
Source push/remote remains owner-only. Weekly usage last checked at 32%; pause at
50% consumed. The inline GSD fallback claims no typed agent-role guarantees.
